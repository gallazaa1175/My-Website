Self-Improvement

Check out the following 6 tips to a happier you!


  • Exercising Regularly: Everyone knows exercise is a cornerstone of health and fitness — but did you know that even just a few minutes of daily exercise can dramatically boost your ability to be happier? Action Steps: Aim to have a combination of aerobic, stretching, and jogging exercises for 30–60 minutes every day.
  • Expressing Gratitude: Multiple peer-reviewed studies have shown that frequently expressing gratitude increases happiness and fulfillment, reduces stress and depression, and even relieves symptoms of illness. Action Steps: Keep a gratitude journal and write down at least one thing you're grateful for at the start of each day.
  • Getting Enough Sleep: Multiple studies show that your quality of sleep directly affects your quality of life. A lack of sleep wreaks havoc on your brain, including mood control and brain health. Action Steps: Allow yourself to have at least 7 hours of quality sleep each night.
  • Focusing On Relationships:
    "We are happy when we have family, we are happy when we have friends and almost all the other things we think make us happy are actually just ways of getting more family and friends."
    Remember — not staying in touch with loved ones is one of the biggest regrets reported later in life. Action Steps: Remember to stay in touch with your family and friends regularly!
  • Meditation: Meditation helps cultivate a calmer, more positive, and happier mindset throughout your entire day. Action Steps: Start meditating for 5 minutes a day, either in the morning or before bed.
  • Eat Sensibly: Reduce portions, eat slowly, choose healthy snacks (avoid high-fat or high-sugar items like cookies, chips, and sodas), reduce fat intake, and drink plenty of water.
